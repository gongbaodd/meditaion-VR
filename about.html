Still Places is a free meditation space for VR headsets and the web. It is meant for relaxation and mindfulness practice and is not a medical treatment.

Places, sounds and fonts

Every panorama comes from Poly Haven (CC0). Bells and ambience are synthesized in the browser unless a recording is listed below. The guidance text is original to this project.

These organisations offer free guided meditations. Their recordings are not included in this app.

  • UCLA Mindful — short guided meditations in many languages.
  • Palouse Mindfulness — a free, complete 8-week MBSR course.
  • Free Mindfulness Project — guided exercises and bell timers.
  • Audio Dharma and Dharma Seed — talks and guided meditations.
  • Medito — a free, non-profit meditation app.
